# Replication Package for Ramos and Vital (2025) Journal of Family Research

**Paper:** [Research note: Merry now, marry later? Initial labor market conditions and marital intentions in the Philippines](https://ubp.uni-bamberg.de/jfr/index.php/jfr/article/view/1226)

## About
This folder contains the replication .do files including a data processing step and a data analysis steps. The researchers must first specify their respective working directories in the allocated space on the do file.

These do files were written and ran using Stata 19.5/SE for Windows. 

## Files
```text
1_processing.do
```
 contains data wrangling steps

```text
2_analysis.do
```
contains the codes to replicate the figures and tables as they appear on the research note. The replication codes to the tables and figures in the online supplement are also in this do file.

## Data Availability Statement

The Graduate Tracer Survey microdata used in this study is available by request from the Commission on Higher Education of the Philippines.
